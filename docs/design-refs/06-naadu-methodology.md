# Naadu (by Revaik): Design Methodology

A breakdown of the visual and storytelling system used by **@naadu.revaik** (*നാട് | Naadu: "World for malayalees"*), an AI-powered Malayalam channel that retells Kerala history as cinematic short reels.

Use this as a playbook to make content in the same style.

---

## 1. Core Concept

| Pillar | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Hyper-local subject** | Every post is about Kerala: its history, places, firsts and lost legends. |
| **Native language first** | Headlines and captions are in Malayalam. English is used only as a small secondary line. |
| **Cinematic AI visuals** | Every cover is an AI-generated, film-still-quality scene. No stock photos, no flat graphics. |
| **Curiosity hooks** | Titles are framed as surprising claims or questions, not plain facts. |
| **Series format** | Content is grouped into numbered series (e.g. *കേരള ചരിത്രം · 12/20: Kerala history in 20 reels*). |

---

## 2. Cover / Thumbnail Anatomy

Every reel cover follows the same 3-layer template:

```
┌─────────────────────────────┐
│  MALAYALAM HEADLINE (white, │  ← Layer 1: Bold Malayalam title, 1–2 lines, centered
│  bold, centered, 1–2 lines) │
│   ENGLISH SUBTITLE IN CAPS  │  ← Layer 2: Tiny English tagline, yellow/gold, letter-spaced
│                             │
│                             │
│    CINEMATIC AI SCENE       │  ← Layer 3: Full-bleed hero image
│   (golden hour / dusk /     │
│    dramatic sky)            │
│                             │
└─────────────────────────────┘
```

### Layer 1: Malayalam Headline
- **Color:** Pure white, often with a soft dark shadow or glow so it reads over any background.
- **Weight:** Heavy or bold Malayalam display font.
- **Position:** Top-centre, in the sky or negative space of the image.
- **Length:** 2–6 words. Punchy.
- Examples seen:
  - റോം തകർന്നത് കൊടുങ്ങല്ലൂർ കാരണം? (*Rome fell because of Kodungallur?*)
  - കൊല്ലത്തിന്റെ വിരലടയാളം (*Kollam's fingerprint*)
  - കേരളത്തിന്റെ ഇരട്ട സഹോദരൻ (*Kerala's twin brother*)
  - 1341: ഒരു പ്രളയം കേരളത്തിന്റെ കിരീടം കൊണ്ടുപോയ വർഷം (*1341: the year a flood took Kerala's crown*)
  - കേരളത്തെ കാത്ത മതിൽ (*The wall that protected Kerala*)

### Layer 2: English Subtitle
- **Style:** ALL CAPS, small size, wide letter-spacing.
- **Color:** Warm yellow, gold or amber, sometimes on a thin dark strip.
- **Role:** Gives non-Malayalam viewers and search context (e.g. *"HOW THE 1341 FLOOD…"*, *"THE WESTERN GHATS: THE WALL THAT SAVED THE…"*).

### Layer 3: Hero Image
- Full-bleed, vertical (9:16 reel, cropped to 4:5 or 1:1 in the grid).
- The subject sits in the **lower 2/3**. The **upper 1/3 is kept clear** (sky, haze, darkness) for text.

## 3. Visual Language

### Lighting
- **Golden hour and sunset** are the dominant look (warm orange, amber, gold).
- **Blue hour and night** scenes use moonlight, oil lamps (*nilavilakku*) and starfields.
- **Dramatic contrast:** a single light source, volumetric rays, glowing elements (e.g. a glowing golden path, light leaking through a window).

### Colour Palette
| Role | Colours |
|---|---|
| Warm primary | Saffron orange `#E8892B`, Gold `#F2B33D`, Amber `#C9731F` |
| Kerala greens | Paddy green `#5FA83A`, Palm/forest `#2F5D2A` |
| Earth | Laterite red `#A8452A`, Terracotta `#8C3B22` |
| Cool accent | Backwater teal `#2E8C9A`, Night blue `#14233F` |
| Text | White `#FFFFFF`, Accent yellow `#F5C542` |

### Composition Patterns
- **Aerial / drone wide shots:** backwaters, paddy fields, coastline, Western Ghats.
- **Low-angle heroic shots:** ships, temples, statues (e.g. Jatayu).
- **Interior chiaroscuro:** dark rooms lit by a lamp or window (*kalari*, studies, palm-leaf manuscripts).
- **Before/after or symbolic objects:** a sprouting plant, a glowing ring, Earth from space.
- **Leading lines:** rivers, roads and glowing paths guide the eye toward the subject.

### Recurring Kerala Motifs
Coconut palms · houseboats and *uru* (wooden ships) · laterite step-wells and temple tanks · Kerala-style sloped tiled roofs · *nilavilakku* lamps · Western Ghats mist · Arabian Sea coastline · Kalaripayattu silhouettes.

## 4. Reel Structure (Inside the Video)

Based on the *1341 flood / Muziris* reel:

1. **Hook (0–3s):** A big question or year in large text over striking imagery.
   *"2018? Remember the 2018 floods?"* over a rotating Earth.
2. **Bridge to the present:** Connect the history to something the viewer lived through (the 2018 floods, Kochi airport, etc.).
3. **Story reveal:** Narrate the historical event with AI scenes, maps, archival images and satellite data.
4. **Twist or payoff:** The surprising consequence (*the flood destroyed Muziris but created Kochi*).
5. **Close:** A question to the viewer that invites comments.

**Production stack (per credits):** AI voice-over, sound effects, animation and cover art by Revaik, mixed with **public-domain and CC-licensed archival sources** (NASA imagery, old maps, Wikimedia museum photos).

On-screen subtitles: Malayalam, white, centred, mid-to-lower frame, on a subtle dark backing.

## 5. Caption Formula

```
[Hook question in Malayalam] + emoji
                                        ← blank line
[2–3 line story summary in Malayalam]
                                        ← blank line
[Engagement question: "Have you been to ___? Comment 👇"]
[Share CTA: "Send to friends & family groups who love history. Save it!"]
                                        ← blank line
[One-line English summary]
                                        ← blank line
[Series tag: കേരള ചരിത്രം · 12/20 · Kerala history in 20 reels]
                                        ← blank line
Credits:
• Source 1 · licence
• Source 2 · licence
• Voice, sound effects and animation: Revaik
• Cover art: Revaik
                                        ← blank line
#KeralaHistory #Topic #Place #Malayalam
```

Key moves:
- **Three CTAs:** comment, share (specifically to *family WhatsApp groups*), save.
- **Transparent credits** build trust and keep sources legally clean.
- **Bilingual:** Malayalam for the audience, English for reach and SEO.
- **Hashtags:** 4–6 focused tags, no spam.

## 6. Profile and Grid Strategy

- **Bio:** Brand name in English and Malayalam, a one-line mission, "Powered by Revaik", an inquiry email and a link.
- **Avatar:** A minimal monogram ("R" on a warm brown or gold gradient).
- **Pinned posts:** 3 of the strongest hooks pinned at the top.
- **Grid consistency:** Every tile shares the same text template and warm cinematic grade, so the grid reads as one series.
- **Frequency:** A finite numbered series (20 reels) creates a binge and completion urge.

## 7. Content Ideation Framework

Topics fall into repeatable angles:

| Angle | Example titles |
|---|---|
| **Kerala and world history** | Rome fell because of Kodungallur? |
| **Firsts and records** | First church, mosque and synagogue · Kochi Airport |
| **Hidden origins** | Origin of the *pooja* · Kerala's twin brother |
| **Disasters and turning points** | 1341 flood · The rain timeline |
| **Geography as hero** | The Western Ghats: the wall that saved Kerala |
| **Myth meets fact** | Jatayu · El Dorado in Thrissur? |
| **Dates** | November 1: Kerala rising from the sea |

**Title rule:** *Familiar place + unexpected claim + curiosity gap.*
