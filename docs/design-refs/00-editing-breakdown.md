## 1. ആമസോൺ നദിയിലേക്ക്! (Amazon river), 1:54

**What happens on screen**

- **0–25s:** a green jungle with layered tree silhouettes (parallax) and a jeep driving through it. Around 24s the jeep tips over a bank.
- **25–60s:** a hard change to a dark blue night scene with mist over the river and the jeep in silhouette.
- **60–114s:** an anaconda (a patterned silhouette) slides through the frame at water level, and the scene slowly gets darker.

**Editing notes**

- It is almost a one-shot video. The camera slowly pans while foreground, middle and background layers move at different speeds (parallax).
- Colour does the storytelling: bright green means safe, dark teal means danger.
- A small "CINEMAGIC" watermark sits at the top centre. The caption says "Watch the full video on YouTube", so the reel is a teaser cut from the long video.

**How to recreate it:** in After Effects, put 4–5 silhouette layers on 3D layers and animate a single slow camera move. Free vector silhouette packs (Freepik, Vecteezy) work well. Add fog with a Fractal Noise layer at low opacity.

## 2. കേരളം മുങ്ങിയ ആ രാത്രി (Kerala floods), 0:47

**What happens on screen**

- **0–5s:** a town street in heavy rain, with a "2018 May" date stamp (time and place set immediately).
- **5–10s:** a house with lit windows as water rises.
- **10–15s:** an aerial "map" view of a winding flooded river.
- **15–25s:** people crowding onto balconies and stairs, holding red emergency bags.
- **25s:** a press crowd with cameras and flashes and a "100" counter.
- **29–37s:** a silhouette of a man watching TV news, then a close-up of the remote.
- **41s:** a black pause.
- **45s:** end card: "Out now on YouTube".

**Editing notes**

- A **rain particle layer runs over every shot**. It ties all the scenes together and hides the cuts.
- The palette is navy and blue only, with **red as the one accent** (bags, lights) to draw the eye.
- There is a short black pause before the CTA.
- This is the most-liked recent reel. An emotional local story plus a date hook works best.

**How to recreate it:** flat vector scenes (Illustrator or Freepik), each with a slow zoom. Add CC Rainfall in After Effects, or a rain overlay in CapCut. Put a date in the corner of the opening shot.

## 3. മിന്നുന്ന നക്ഷത്രങ്ങൾ (Twinkling stars), 0:51

**What happens on screen**

- **0–11s:** a grandma and child sitting on a Kerala veranda at night under the Moon. The child asks the question.
- **11–20s:** the camera zooms up to one star, and a dotted light ray travels down to a tiny house on Earth.
- **20–32s:** the ray passes through coloured atmosphere layers and **wobbles** (the explanation).
- **32–40s:** an analogy: a pebble seen through moving water looks shaky.
- **40–46s:** back to grandma and child.
- **46–51s:** the camera zooms into a star sparkle that fills the screen, which works as the end transition.

**Editing notes**

- The structure is **story frame, explanation, analogy, back to the story**.
- Diagrams use the **same art style** as the story, so it never feels like a lecture.
- The ending zoom-in transition makes the reel loop nicely.

## 4. നീങ്ങിക്കൊണ്ടിരിക്കുന്ന നമ്മൾ (We are moving), 1:13

**What happens on screen**

- **0–9s:** grandma and boy on the veranda under the stars.
- **9–21s:** a close-up of their feet, then the **boy at a train window** watching paddy fields go by at sunset. This is the analogy: you don't feel the train moving.
- **21–27s:** Earth rotating in space.
- **27–40s:** the spiral galaxy.
- **40–52s:** back to the train with the window shutter closed. You still can't feel the motion.
- **52–64s:** the veranda, then the Sun with Earth orbiting.
- **64–73s:** back to the veranda.

**Editing notes**

- The images are **AI-painted in a warm, Ghibli-like style** (Kerala houses, mundu and saree, coconut trees), then animated with slow pushes and light motion.
- The editing **cuts between the human analogy and space visuals** several times.
- Every 6–10s there is a new shot, and each shot has a slow zoom.

**How to recreate it:** generate stills with Midjourney or another AI image tool using the same character description every time, so the characters stay consistent. Animate them with Kling or Runway image-to-video using gentle motion (2–5s clips). Use NASA footage or AI renders for Earth and the galaxy.

- **17–25s:** the camera pulls back and one big virus appears at the bottom.

**Editing notes**

**How to recreate it:** Blender (free virus models on Sketchfab), or an AI video prompt such as *"cinematic dark blue microscopic space full of floating virus particles, slow camera tilt, volumetric light, 9:16"*.

- **15–23s:** the sky slowly fades to night and a moon appears.
- **23–50s:** a static night scene with stars twinkling.

**Editing notes**

## 10. നിഗൂഢമായ ഗ്രഹം (Mysterious planet, GJ 1214 b), the planet animation, @_cinemagic_official_, 1:00

**What happens on screen**

- **0–10s:** a black starfield with a blue planet (a Neptune-like sphere) sliding in from the side.
- **10–15s:** a small Earth appears beside it for a **size comparison**, with a "light years: 48" label.
- **15–20s:** an underwater shot with light rays (the "ocean planet" idea).
- **20–45s:** Earth and the planet side by side with **infographic text**: "70% of the surface of Earth", "0.0005% of Earth's mass" and the planet's name "GJ 1214 b".
- **45–60s:** a 3D ocean surface with a white sun on a teal-to-yellow horizon, and the camera gliding over the waves.

**Editing notes**

- This is the **motion infographic** style: a plain black starfield, planets as simple spheres that **slide, scale and swap places**, and short English data labels typed or faded on.
- Numbers appear one by one (counter animation) as the voiceover says them.
- It ends on a realistic 3D "what it looks like there" shot as the emotional payoff.
- The Cinemagic logo sits in the top-right corner the whole time.

**How to recreate it**

1. **Planets:** use free NASA planet textures (or Solar System Scope textures) on a sphere. In After Effects, use the built-in CC Sphere effect, or Blender. Add a slow Y-rotation.
2. **Starfield:** a black solid with CC Star Burst or a free star overlay.
3. **Comparison:** keyframe Position and Scale so Earth slides in and the planet grows (ease in/out, F9).
4. **Data text:** Montserrat or a similar font, white, 40–60px, fading in word by word (Text Animator) or with a "Number counter" preset.
5. **Ocean shot:** use a Blender ocean modifier, or an AI video prompt such as *"alien ocean planet surface, endless dark waves, small white sun on horizon, cinematic, 9:16"*.

## Their overall editing format

### Four visual styles they rotate

1. **Flat vector, limited palette**: history and news (French Revolution, Kerala floods, Butterfly Effect)
2. **AI-painted "Ammoomma & child" series**: everyday science questions (Moon, stars, motion)
3. **Space / 3D motion infographic**: planets, viruses, the universe
4. **Single calm scene + quote**: The Great Life motivation

### Common rules across all of them

- **9:16 vertical**, with the main subject in the centre and the watermark logo in a top corner.
- **Movement is constant but slow:** every shot has a zoom, pan or parallax. There are almost no static frames and no jump cuts.
- **Voiceover leads.** Visuals change when the narration changes idea, not to the beat.
- **One accent colour** per reel (red in the floods reel, gold in the stars reel, orange in the French Revolution reel).
- **Overlay layers** (rain, stars, fog, particles) run across cuts to keep it smooth.
- **Date or place stamp** at the start of history reels ("2018 May", "1960 AMERICA").
- **Endings:** a "Watch the full video on YouTube" card, an animated subscribe button, or a zoom-in transition that loops back to the start.
- **Long topics become series:** Part 1, 2, 3… with the same title and cover style.

### Reel template you can copy

| Time | Section | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Hook | Strongest visual + question in voiceover (date/place stamp if history) |
| 3–10s | Setup | Characters or scene; slow push-in |
| 10–40s | Explanation | 1 visual per idea, 5–10s each, a diagram in the same art style, an everyday analogy |
| 40–50s | Payoff | Answer + the most beautiful shot (zoom out to space, 3D landscape) |
| 50–60s | End | Back to the opening scene / "Part 2 next" / YouTube CTA |

### Tool stack

| Job | Tool |
|---|---|
| Illustrations | Freepik/Vecteezy vectors, Illustrator, Midjourney or another AI image tool for the painted style |
| Animate images | After Effects (parallax, camera), Kling, Runway or Veo (image to video) |
| Space and 3D | NASA SVS footage, Solar System Scope textures, SpaceEngine, Blender |
| Edit, captions, sound | Premiere Pro or CapCut; ambient music; whoosh and rain sound effects |
| Voice | Your own Malayalam narration with a calm storytelling tone (the brand's main asset) |
