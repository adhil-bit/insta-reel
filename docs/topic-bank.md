1. **Kannur's Vault No. 38** (fresh 2026 story, twist ending)
   Grew into fears of a hidden crime: police investigations, scientific tests, even a reopened decade-old missing-person case.
   After 12 days the vault turned out to have no hidden body and no crime scene.
   → Template D (myth vs fact). News story: credit the news source, no private names, no accusations.
2. **Kerala University's "haunted" pond, Karyavattom** (myth-buster)
   A pond on the Karyavattom campus was abandoned because people believed it was haunted by a young woman's spirit, until a group's effort turned it into a freshwater source and a student hangout.
   **Upgrade (Hymavathi pond twist):** a parapsychologist says the ghost story merged two separate events. A girl named Hymavathi accidentally drowned in a different pond 2 km away, and an unrelated death on campus years later got mixed in.
   → Template D. A real person died: be respectful, no dramatised death, consider not naming her on screen.
3. **Chelembra, Kerala's "Money Heist"**
   Four people from Kozhikode, inspired by the film Dhoom, robbed the Chelembra branch of South Malabar Gramin Bank. The bank was on a first floor; the gang broke in through a hole in its floor. They took 80.199 kg of gold and ₹25 lakh; it came to light on December 31, 2007.
   → Template A/B (twist: how they were caught). Don't glamorise the crime; no names of the accused; no film clips or posters.
4. **The Kannur treasure pot (2024)**
   July 2024: women workers digging a rainwater pit in Chengalayi thought a buried pot was a bomb, then feared black magic. Inside: gold beads, lockets, medallions, rings, earrings and silver-like coins. Reports put the 18th-century coins at around 200 years old.
   Mystery: who buried it, and why? → Template D, strong Kannur local hook.
5. **The last Jews of Kochi**
   Kochi's Paradesi Jewish community once had 2,000 members. When Sarah Cohen died in 2019 at 96, only two were left. The synagogue couldn't hold prayers for years because it lacked the required 10 men.
   Emotional hook: she was cared for by a Muslim man, Thaha Ibrahim. → Respectful, warm tone.
6. **The valley where leaves don't fall: Ilaveezhapoonchira**
   "Ela Veezha Poonchira" (Malayalam film, released 15 July 2022) — use the film name in the hook if it works (name only, no clips/posters).
   The name means "the valley where leaves don't fall". Twist: the hills around it have no trees, so there are no leaves to fall. Locals also link it to the Pandavas' exile (legend).
