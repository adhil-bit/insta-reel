# voice.md: Unexplored Truths (@unexplored.truths) · Instagram

Read by the ig-* skills. Copy to `~/.claude/instagram/voice.md` when using them in Claude Code.

## Who I am talking to
One Malayali, 18-35, scrolling Reels or Shorts on a phone, curious but impatient: they stop for a
surprising fact about a place or thing they thought they knew, and they share it if it makes them
look smart or proud of Kerala/India. Many have never heard of the place in the video.

## How I actually talk
Narration is spoken in conversational Kerala Malayalam, like explaining something amazing to a friend:
"ഒരു രസമുള്ള കാര്യം പറയട്ടെ?", "അറിയാമോ?", "അല്ലേ?", "കേട്ടോ". Short sentences. Everyday
Manglish words are fine (കറന്റ്, റിയൽ). English is used only for the big on-screen headlines.

## Words I never use
"Did you know" as an opener, "fact:", "mind-blowing", "you won't believe", "let's dive in",
"subscribe" in the first 30 seconds, clickbait the video can't pay off.

## Words I do use
"നമ്മുടെ" (our) for Kerala/India topics, a question to the viewer in the hook, "എന്തുകൊണ്ടെന്നോ?" before
the twist, and a closing line that invites a share ("Send this to someone from ...").

## What I will not claim
Any number, date or "first/largest/only" that isn't in the reel's fact list with a source. Legends
are said as "പറയപ്പെടുന്നു" (it is said). No claims about religion or politics beyond plain history.
No real people's faces, no gore.

## My format
Vertical 9:16, 30-45 seconds, no face on camera, animated illustrations. Opens on the hook with the
cover visual (globe zoom only when the location itself is the surprise). One English headline per
scene at the top, Malayalam subtitles at the bottom, 5 scenes, a 2-second follow card at the end.
Gemini narrators: Charon, Despina, Enceladus, Callirrhoe, Puck, Zephyr.
